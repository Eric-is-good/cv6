# Hex Dominion

A pure web, turn-based 4X prototype inspired by the strategic loop of Civilization-style games.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play. No build step or backend is required.

## Features

- Procedural hex map with terrain, resources, fog of war, and tile ownership.
- Player units: settlers, warriors, scouts, builders, spearmen, and archers.
- City founding, growth, production queues, buildings, and tile improvements.
- Science and civic tracks with unlocks.
- Simple AI civilization that expands, researches, produces units, and attacks.
- Domination, science, culture, and score victory checks.
- Local browser save/load through `localStorage`.

## Controls

- Click a unit or city to select it.
- Click highlighted tiles to move selected units.
- Click adjacent enemy units or cities to attack.
- Use the bottom action bar for founding cities, improving tiles, healing, and fortifying.
- End Turn advances production, research, growth, AI actions, and victory checks.
